Who we are

Mini Mir is made by GeoGianni Games LLC, a limited liability company registered in Colorado, United States. We are responsible for the data described here.

Contact: privacy@geogianni.com

1Gameplay statistics

When you first start Mini Mir we show you a short notice about this policy. After that, the game sends us statistics about how it is played, for example:

  • your progress through the story and the jobs, and the choices you make in them;
  • game stats such as play time, money, lines and riders;
  • technical details: game version, operating system, hardware basics, settings and performance.

We use them to balance the game, find where players get stuck and, in a later update, show you how other players chose.

By default the statistics carry your Steam ID and a random install id, so we can tell your sessions apart and answer you if you ask about your data. In Settings you can turn on “Anonymize my data”: statistics are then sent without your Steam ID or any other id that links them to you. Everything else stays the same.

There is no option to switch statistics off completely while playing; anonymizing them is the choice we offer.

2Why we're allowed to use this data

We use gameplay statistics and error reports because we have a legitimate interest in balancing and fixing the game. We use the contact details you add to an F10 report only because you agree to it (the consent box), and you can withdraw that at any time by writing to us.

3Automatic error reports

When the game hits an error or crashes, it can send an error report: the error and its stack trace, a short tail of the game's log with your system user name removed from file paths, the game version and platform, the scene, and a random install id. It contains no account, device id or IP address. Each distinct error is sent once per session, up to 10 reports per session.

You can turn this off in Settings › Gameplay › “Send automatic error reports”. Turning it off also deletes any error reports still waiting to be sent.

4Bug reports and feedback you send (F10)

Pressing F10 opens a report form. Nothing is sent until you press Send. An online report holds:

  • what you typed (type, title, description, steps to reproduce);
  • one screenshot of the game, if you leave the Screenshot box ticked;
  • where you were in the game (day, time, mission);
  • your contact details, only if you type them and tick the consent box.

The game log and a snapshot of the game state stay in the copy saved on your computer; they are not sent online. A copy of every report is saved on your computer, in the game's Reports folder.

5Steam

Mini Mir runs through Steam. Achievements, Steam stats and cloud saves are handled by Valve under Steam's own Privacy Policy and Subscriber Agreement. We see what Steamworks shows developers (for example, which achievements players have unlocked).

6What stays on your computer

Your save games, settings and the local copies of your reports stay on your computer (and in Steam Cloud, if you use it). We never see them unless you send them to us.

7Where your data goes and how long we keep it

Statistics and reports go to our own server, run by GeoGianni Games on Railway (server) and Neon (database), in the United States. If you play from outside the US, including the EU and UK, your data is transferred to the US. Our providers handle it for us under data processing terms that include the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your IP address is used only to limit spam: it is stored in hashed form, never in the clear.

  • Bug and error reports we don't act on are deleted after 30 days.
  • Reports we turn into work items are kept until the issue is closed, then deleted 90 days later.
  • Gameplay statistics are kept for 12 months. After that we keep only daily totals that can't be linked to anyone.

We don't sell or share your data. People who help us make the game (for example, playtest coordinators) may see reports under the same rules.

8Your choices and rights

  • Anonymize statistics: Settings › “Anonymize my data”.
  • Stop automatic error reports: Settings › Gameplay.
  • Leave out the screenshot or your contact details on any F10 report.
  • Ask us what we hold about your Steam ID, or ask us to delete it: write to privacy@geogianni.com with your Steam ID. Anonymized statistics and reports without contact details can't be linked back to you, so we can't find or delete those.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you may also have the right to object to how we use your data and to complain to your data protection authority.

9Children

Mini Mir contains crime and violence and is not meant for children. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16.

10Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and say so in the patch notes. Big changes will be shown in the game before they apply.
